The company operates primarily in the United States.

Title: Acme Artificial Intelligence Products

Acme Corporation develops several artificial intelligence products.

DOCUMENT 004
Title: AcmeAI Security

AcmeAI uses retrieval-augmented generation for some enterprise applications.

The system retrieves information from authorized enterprise documents before generating responses.

AcmeAI applications can enforce access-control policies before documents are retrieved.

The company recommends that customers avoid placing unnecessary sensitive personal information into prompts.

DOCUMENT 010
Title: Acme Corporation Security Policy

Acme Corporation recommends that AI applications use the principle of least privilege.

Tools should only receive the permissions necessary to perform their intended functions.

High-impact or irreversible actions should require additional validation or human approval.

DOCUMENT 011
Title: AcmeAI Limitations

Acme Corporation acknowledges that generative AI systems can produce incorrect or unsupported information.

Retrieval can improve access to relevant information but does not guarantee that generated responses are factually correct.

Applications should validate important information before relying on it for high-impact decisions.
